Priority: higher numbers are preferred first. 0 means neutral/default (not disabled).
Calls/Second: max average request rate allowed for this provider. Keep below your vendor quota.
Capabilities: the wizard and provider tests detect chain connectivity and historical `eth_getLogs` support automatically. Range-limited endpoints are enabled when their tested limit is usable.
Runtime state vs. verification: runtime state reflects passive live traffic and backoff. Timestamped capability cards are the active endpoint tests.
